Practice the difficult conversation before the patient is real.

Talk with a virtual patient by voice. The chart opens as you earn it. Feedback is tied to the evidence you actually collected.

From case to feedback in four steps

1. Author

Pick a patient, publish if you edited, and start the visit.

2. Interview

Hold a spoken conversation. Facts open by trust, question, and chart section.

3. Reconcile

Patient statements and chart records disagree on purpose. Find it and document.

4. Review

Hand off in SBAR, then see a score for each objective with the evidence behind it.

The model never decides what is medically true

Every patient line comes from an approved fact in the published profile. Missing data stays missing. Unsupported answers fall back to “I'm not sure.” All data is synthetic.
